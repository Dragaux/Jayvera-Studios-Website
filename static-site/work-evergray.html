Evergray

An awareness and donor site for senior shelter-pet adoption, built to connect visitors with The Grey Muzzle Organization.

Visit live site →
Year 2026 Status live Stack Node.js,Express,SQLite,Resend
01The problem

Senior shelter pets are consistently the hardest animals to get adopted, and awareness for the organizations that support them is limited.

02The build

Evergray is a focused awareness and donor site built around senior pet adoption, with a donor management system, an admin dashboard, and a GoFundMe fundraiser linked directly to The Grey Muzzle Organization. Resend handles email so donors and site admins both stay in the loop.

03Where it stands

Deployed and live at evergray.up.railway.app, running on Node.js/Express with a SQLite backend on Railway.
